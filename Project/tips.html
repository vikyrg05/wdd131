Healthy Study Habits

Creating a consistent study schedule helps students manage time effectively. Taking short breaks between study sessions can improve concentration and prevent mental exhaustion.

Sleep and Rest

Getting enough sleep is essential for memory, focus, and emotional balance. Students should aim for 7-8 hours of sleep each night to maintain good health.

Stress Management

Practicing deep breathing, light exercise, or mindfulness techniques can reduce stress levels and improve overall well-being.
